Assumptions & limitations

  • No German sales exist. Units are estimated from 78 weeks of NL/DK/SE sales, deduplicated and annualised, multiplied by the 52% relevant-category share and a 50% entry-scale assumption, then adjusted with German survey acceptance and channel intent. The city view allocates that scenario by city share, assumes the same response in every city, and rounds city units to hundreds. See the methodology in the README.
  • Only three prices were tested (€1.79, €2.19, €2.59). Slider positions between them are linear interpolations — exploratory only, and never used for the recommendation.
  • Contribution uses channel economics (what LUMEN nets after retailer, distributor, payment and fulfilment costs), not shelf revenue. It excludes fixed and launch costs and is not net profit. Units are rounded to hundreds; money is calculated from those rounded units.
  • Seasonality comes from the monthly demand index; the launch window is mid-April to early May, ahead of the June–August peak; annual totals are not increased. Survey intent may not become purchases.
