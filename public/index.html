Illustrative shares assume no channel cannibalization (channels do not take sales from one another). This is not an optimized allocation or a validated combined-sales forecast.

How channel shares are calculated

Each channel’s estimated units are divided by the sum across DTC, Retail and Gym & Office, at the recommended common tested price. Rounding makes shares total 100%. Balance profit & reach ranks launch options; its weights do not directly allocate sales. Shares change with the selected city or recommended price. Incomplete data or a zero total makes shares unavailable.

Methodology, assumptions & limitations

Balance profit & reach scores each valid tested channel/price combination for the selected city. Each metric is normalized as (value − minimum) / (maximum − minimum) across that same candidate set. Score = profitability weight × normalized contribution + market-share weight × normalized units.

Missing or incomplete scenarios are excluded. If a metric is equal for every candidate, its normalized value is zero for everyone. Exact ties use the case's existing channel order, then tested-price order. At 0% or 100%, Balance profit & reach reproduces the corresponding single-objective recommendation. Scores compare preferences, not future outcomes.

  • Only three prices were tested (€1.79, €2.19, €2.59). Slider positions between them are linear interpolations — exploratory only, and never used for the recommendation.
  • Estimated contribution uses channel economics (what LUMEN nets after retailer, distributor, payment and fulfilment costs), not shelf revenue. It excludes fixed and launch costs and is not net profit. Units are rounded to hundreds; money is calculated from those rounded units.
  • Seasonality comes from the monthly demand index; the launch window is mid-April to early May, ahead of the June–August peak; annual totals are not increased. Survey intent may not become purchases.
