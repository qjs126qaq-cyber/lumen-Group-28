Assumptions & limitations

No German LUMEN sales exist. These are planning scenarios, not forecasts or guaranteed returns.

We remove duplicate sales records from the Netherlands, Denmark and Sweden, then convert 78 weeks into a yearly benchmark. We scale this by the relevant German category share (—) and a cautious — entry assumption, then adjust for German price acceptance and channel preference.

Units are rounded to the nearest 100. Retail revenue is units × shelf price; contribution is units × tested channel contribution per can. Contribution is not net profit and does not deduct all launch or fixed costs.

Survey intent may not become purchases, and established markets may not reflect a new German launch. Seasonality guides timing; it does not increase the annual total. Actual distribution, competition and launch execution can change the outcome.
